Professional life.
Thoughtfully designed.

The Badger Co. is an independent company building technology and design for professional life. Our primary product is BadgerFit—a structured tool bringing clarity to everyday footwear fit and foot comfort.

Brand & Product

The Badger Co. and BadgerFit.

The Badger Co. represents our broader brand mission: creating deliberate products where modern technology meets daily routine.

BadgerFit is our current software product. It addresses the friction people encounter in everyday footwear through two structured experiences: Fit Check for specific shoe discomfort, and Foot Check for general foot comfort and prolonged standing.

The Longer View

Better questions.
Better products.

Over time, we want consented research and validated fit patterns to inform the physical footwear and products we design.

That is an intentional long-term direction, not a claim about a finished physical shoe collection. Today, our focus is perfecting Fit Check and Foot Check inside BadgerFit, while advancing reference validation for future measurement tools.
