Before you start

Straight answers.

What can I use today?

The BadgerFit beta includes Shoe Fit Check for discomfort in a specific pair. The app opens at sign-in; use the shoe-discomfort path after entering the app.

Which shoes are you focusing on?

Our focus is dress shoes, dress boots, and everyday sneakers. The current beta still lists additional footwear categories; dedicated subtype guidance is not yet built.

Do I need a photo?

No. Shoe Fit Check can use your reported experience without a photo or scan.

Does this diagnose foot problems?

No. It provides educational fit guidance and can show a safety message based on reported symptoms. It does not diagnose a condition or replace professional care.

Are my shoe cases saved across devices?

Fit Check case history is currently stored in the browser on the device you use. It is not yet synced across devices and can be lost if browser data is cleared. Other account data is handled separately; see our privacy notice.
