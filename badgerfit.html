BadgerFit / Product Overview

Less guessing.
A clearer next step for shoes and feet.

BadgerFit is the footwear comfort tool by The Badger Co. Built for professional life, it combines structured questions, saved personal history, and plain-language education to help you resolve shoe discomfort and daily standing fatigue.

The Two Core Experiences

Fit Check for shoes.
Foot Check for feet.

BadgerFit establishes clear boundaries between shoe-specific fit issues and broader foot discomfort. Both experiences receive equal prominence inside the product.

01 / Shoe-Specific

Fit Check

For problems with a specific pair of shoes. Tell BadgerFit where your shoe feels wrong and receive actionable, structured next steps.

  • Tailored to shoe categories: dress shoes, dress boots, everyday sneakers
  • Identifies heel slipping, toe-box pressure, rubbing, and lace pinch
  • Checks width, arch room, and internal volume mismatches
  • Saves results directly into your footwear history in My Shoes
Dedicated Fit Check page →
02 / Everyday Foot Comfort

Foot Check

For broader foot discomfort not tied to one pair. Evaluate how your feet feel across standing routines, long workdays, and daily movement.

  • Independent of any single shoe in your rotation
  • Captures daily standing hours, floor surfaces, and shift timing
  • Identifies fatigue patterns, aching arches, and recovery needs
  • Records your ongoing comfort trend in My Foot

How It Works / Contextual Flow

From friction to clarity
in three steps.

BadgerFit replaces unguided online searches and blind sizing guesses with a transparent, rule-guided assessment.

Step 01

Choose your path

Select Fit Check if troubleshooting a particular shoe, or Foot Check if addressing daily foot fatigue and standing discomfort.

Step 02

Answer structured questions

Describe what you feel: location, timing, footwear details, and daily standing conditions. No photo or camera scan needed.

Step 03

Read your 5-part result

Review clear observations, lowest-risk next steps, tools to consider, mistakes to avoid, and medical safety guidance.

The Result Architecture

Five clear parts.
Zero guesswork.

A BadgerFit result is built to be actionable immediately, with clear boundaries on what to try and what to avoid.

Instead of assuming every dress shoe is narrow or that sizing up is always the cure, advice directly reflects your answers and the mechanics of your footwear.

01 / Assessment

What we noticed

Plain-language analysis connecting your answers to likely fit, volume, or standing load factors.

02 / Action

Try first

A practical, low-risk starting step (re-lacing technique, break-in adjustment, or sock selection).

03 / Support

Helpful Tools

Supportive fitting aids, non-diagnostic inserts, or contextual recovery ideas that may assist.

04 / Caution

What to avoid

Common pitfalls (like blindly sizing up or overtightening laces) that could exacerbate the pressure.

05 / Boundary

Safety guidance

Clear red-flag boundaries advising when to stop adjusting fit and consult a healthcare professional.

Before You Start

Straight answers.

What is available in the BadgerFit beta today?

The beta includes Fit Check for specific shoe discomfort, Foot Check for daily foot fatigue, saved history in My Shoes and My Foot, pattern summaries in My Fit Preferences, and our educational resources.

Which footwear types does Fit Check focus on?

Our primary focus is dress shoes, dress boots, and everyday sneakers—the core rotation of professional life. Dedicated subtype guidance continues to expand.

Do I need a camera, photo, or scan?

No. BadgerFit relies on your reported observations, footwear details, and structured questions. No photo or camera scan is required.

Does BadgerFit diagnose medical conditions?

No. BadgerFit provides educational fit guidance and everyday lifestyle suggestions. It does not diagnose clinical conditions or replace assessment by a healthcare professional.

How is my personal data and account handled?

You can manage and delete individual checks or saved footwear profiles directly within the app. Full account deletion is support-assisted during beta by emailing support@thebadgerco.in. See our privacy notice for details.
