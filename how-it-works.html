Concise Walkthrough

How Badger Fit is being designed to work.

The pre-launch concept is intentionally simple: help people understand their feet, learn what fit or footwear needs may matter, and discover how Badger Fit could help before the next purchase.

Three Steps

The planned journey.

01
Step 01
Understand your feet

Start with the foot itself so the fit question has better context than a shoe size alone.

Step 02
Learn your fit and footwear needs

Translate discomfort, shape and fit signals into something easier to act on.

Step 03
Discover how Badger Fit can help

Use that understanding to make more informed footwear decisions and know what to explore next.

Two Product Paths

Two ways the future experience may begin.

02

Understand your feet

The future scan path is intended to help people build clearer fit context and understand why a single size label often falls short.

Understand one uncomfortable shoe

The planned Fit Check path is intended to help people focus on one shoe problem at a time and discover what may be worth trying next.

Badger Fit is still pre-launch. This walkthrough explains the direction honestly and keeps the promise simple: clearer fit understanding first, richer product detail second, and a waitlist for people who want launch updates.
