Status Update / Exploratory Research

Foot Scan remains on the workbench.

Optical foot measurement and camera capture are paused in research while reference standards are validated. A release date has not been announced.

The Technical Challenge

Why Optical Measurement Is Difficult

Smartphone cameras operate with variable focal lengths, lens distortion, tilt angles, and unpredictable lighting. In footwear fit, a variance of just 3 to 4 millimeters across the ball of the foot is the difference between a comfortable shoe and chronic metatarsal compression. Without physical reference calibration, camera captures easily introduce false confidence.

Our Commitment

No Marketing Before Validation

Many consumer apps launch camera-scanning features as novelty engagement hooks, leaving users with inaccurate sizing recommendations. We have deliberately paused public development of Foot Scan until repeatable millimeter accuracy can be proven against physical caliper benchmarks and verified clinical lasts.

Engineering Ethics

Evidence-led design.
Clear boundaries.

We apply three strict standards to any technology we introduce into The Badger Co. ecosystem.

Technology should reduce friction, not create new confusion through unverified algorithmic assumptions.

01 / Distinction

Observation vs Sensors

We clearly separate what users report about how their shoes feel from what physical sensors measure. Our active tools rely on transparent rules, not opaque AI speculation.

02 / Non-Medical

Strict Non-Diagnostic Boundary

We do not claim our tools diagnose medical conditions. When symptoms suggest injury, nerve irritation, or persistent pathology, we provide immediate safety escalation to healthcare professionals.

03 / Privacy

Consented & Controlled Data

No biometric foot images or scans are captured or stored in the current beta. Personal check history is user-controlled, with deletion of individual checks supported in-app.
