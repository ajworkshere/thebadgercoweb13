Privacy & Data Practices

Privacy Policy

How The Badger Co. handles website information and BadgerFit beta application data. Last updated: 15 October 2026.

Who operates BadgerFit

BadgerFit is operated by The Badger Co., Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh, India. For privacy questions, data access, correction requests, or grievances, contact support@thebadgerco.in.

Website signups & inquiries

The footer signup sends your email address, and the main signup form sends your name, email address and optional message, through FormSubmit to support@thebadgerco.in. We use these details to send product updates and respond to inquiries. To request removal from the update list, email us. BadgerFit beta is already available; signing up for updates is not required to use it.

Website analytics and app data

This public website uses Google Analytics to measure website traffic and engagement. BadgerFit account records and check answers are stored separately from website analytics. The app also uses Google Tag Manager for limited usage events, such as starting or completing a check. The app's event code sends event names only, without account identifiers, email addresses, questionnaire answers, measurements, scan records, or photos in those event payloads.

BadgerFit account data

BadgerFit uses Supabase for authentication and account-backed data storage. Account data can include your sign-in details, profile, confirmation that you are 18 or older, saved shoes, Fit Check answers and results, Foot Check responses, and beta feedback. Foot Check responses may include an optional answer about diabetes history. Historical accounts may also have numeric scan records from earlier testing.

We use this information to provide and secure the service, save relevant account history, respond to support requests, and improve the beta. We do not sell or rent personal data.

Signed-in and device-local storage

When you are signed in, supported account data and saved check history can sync through your BadgerFit account. A save must complete successfully before it is available through the account. Guest records, unsynced drafts, and other device-only data remain in the browser where applicable and may be lost if that browser's data is cleared.

The app also uses local browser storage for sign-in state, drafts, pending saves, and preferences. Clearing local data does not by itself delete your account or cloud-saved records.

Current Scan status

Camera Scan is not available in the public beta. Fit Check and Foot Check use self-reported questionnaire answers and do not require a photo. Historical numeric scan records from earlier testing are separate from these current features.

Educational, non-medical guidance

BadgerFit provides educational shoe-fit and foot-comfort guidance. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent medical conditions. It does not replace professional care. Seek qualified professional advice for persistent pain, injury, numbness, swelling, skin changes, or other medical concerns.

Service providers

Supabase supports account sign-in and database storage. Hosting and email providers support service delivery, and FormSubmit processes website signups and inquiries. These services process information as needed to provide their functions.

Eligibility (18+)

BadgerFit is intended only for adults aged 18 and over. You must confirm that you are 18 or older when creating an account. Do not create or use an account if you are under 18.

Account deletion & data management

In the app, go to Profile → Manage Data → Delete account, review and accept the confirmation, then type DELETE when prompted and confirm. Successful deletion removes your account and associated beta records from active storage. The app then clears account-related data from the current browser and signs you out.

If you cannot access the app, email support@thebadgerco.in from your registered account email to request deletion. We may need to verify account ownership. See Account Deletion for the steps and scope. Website signup emails and support correspondence can be requested separately.

Retention and security

We retain information while needed to provide the service, handle support, maintain security, or meet legal obligations. Account deletion concerns active account records; it does not promise immediate erasure of every infrastructure log, backup, or separate support email. Contact support@thebadgerco.in for questions about retained information.

Account-backed data is handled through Supabase, with access controls in the app and backend. Do not send passwords or sign-in codes in support emails.
