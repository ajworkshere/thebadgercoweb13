Privacy & Data Practices / Beta

Privacy information

How The Badger Co. handles website updates and early BadgerFit beta user data.

Website signups & contact

The website footer and waitlist forms collect your email address (and optional name/message) via FormSubmit directly to support@thebadgerco.in. This data is used solely to provide product updates and access announcements. To update your preferences or request deletion from our mailing list, email support@thebadgerco.in.

BadgerFit data & account deletion

Within the BadgerFit app, your saved footwear entries and check logs are stored in My Shoes and My Foot. You can remove individual shoes or check records at any time directly through the app’s data management screens.

Full account deletion is currently support-assisted during the beta release. To permanently delete your BadgerFit account and all associated profile and history data, send a deletion request to support@thebadgerco.in from your registered account email.

Camera & optical scanning status

Foot Scan (optical camera measurement) is paused while measurement precision is validated in research. The live BadgerFit beta does not capture, process, or store biometric foot photos or optical scan models. All Fit Check and Foot Check recommendations are generated exclusively from structured self-reported symptom questionnaires.

Non-medical boundary

BadgerFit does not provide medical diagnoses, treatment plans, or clinical foot evaluations. Content, tips, and guided movement suggestions are educational footwear comfort aids.
