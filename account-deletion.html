Account Management / Privacy

Delete your BadgerFit account

How to permanently delete your BadgerFit account and all associated personal data.

Overview & developer identity

BadgerFit is developed and operated by The Badger Co., Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh, India. We respect your privacy and give you full control over your personal data. You may delete your account at any time either directly within the app or by submitting a deletion request through this page.

Option 1: In-app self-service deletion (Instant)

If you have the BadgerFit app open or installed, you can delete your account immediately with self-service controls:

  1. Open the BadgerFit app and sign in.
  2. Tap Profile in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Select Manage Data.
  4. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  5. Review the permanent deletion disclosure, type DELETE into the confirmation field, and tap Delete account.

Your authentication credentials and all cloud records are permanently purged from active databases immediately upon confirmation, your active session is terminated, and local browser cache is cleared.

Option 2: Web & email deletion request (Support-assisted)

If you no longer have access to the BadgerFit application, have uninstalled it, or are unable to sign in, you can request full account deletion via email:

To: support@thebadgerco.in

Subject: BadgerFit Account Deletion Request

Required details: Please send the request from the email address registered with your BadgerFit account and state that you wish to permanently delete your account and all associated data.

Send deletion request via email →

Verification process: For security and to protect your account against unauthorized deletion, our support team will verify that the request originated from the email address linked to the registered BadgerFit account before executing deletion.

Processing: Upon verification of identity and account ownership, our team will execute the complete deletion of your account and notify you by email once the process is complete.

Data that is permanently deleted

Whether initiated directly inside the app or through a support request, full account deletion permanently removes:

  • Authentication credentials & identity: Your Supabase Auth user record, email address, password hash, and active session tokens.
  • User profile: Account preferences, display settings, and user metadata (user_profiles).
  • 18+ eligibility confirmation: The verification record of your adult age eligibility (user_eligibility).
  • Saved shoes (My Shoes): All footwear entries in your collection, including brand, model, size, shoe type, and fit notes (shoes).
  • Fit Check assessments: All saved Fit Check case records, reported discomfort areas, timing, severity ratings, and recommendation snapshots (fit_check_cases).
  • Foot Check assessments: All structured Foot Check responses, standing hours, footwear types, and optional diabetes response (assessment_responses).
  • Beta feedback: Feedback submissions or ratings submitted during beta testing (beta_feedback).
  • Legacy scan records: Any historical numeric foot dimension measurements from earlier testing (legacy_scans).
  • Local device storage: Browser authentication tokens, cached assessment drafts, and theme preferences stored in local storage.

Data retention & exceptions

No user data retained: We do not retain copies of your assessments, profile, or shoe collection once your account is deleted.

Technical infrastructure logs: Like most web services, standard non-user-identifiable server access and infrastructure logs are temporarily generated for system health, abuse prevention, and rate limiting. These technical logs automatically expire within 30 days and are never linked back to individual user accounts.
