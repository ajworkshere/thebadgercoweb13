The Starting Point

Something about your shoes or feet feels uncomfortable.

A numbered size label only measures length. It leaves width, shoe shape, heel hold, and everyday standing fatigue unaddressed. When a shoe rubs or feet ache, guessing or sizing up blindly usually creates new problems.

"Sizing up adds length, but it doesn't fix a narrow toe box or a loose heel. Clear answers start by asking better questions."

The Badger Co. Design Principle
Standard Sizing Length measurement alone
BadgerFit Approach Shoe context, fit habits & wear patterns

Troubleshooting A Specific Pair

Fit Check

"Something feels wrong with this pair."

Structured troubleshooting for discomfort with a specific pair of shoes in your rotation.

  • Asks about shoe characteristics (dress shoes, boots, sneakers)
  • Identifies where discomfort occurs (toes, heel, arch, top of foot)
  • Asks what the sensation feels like and when it occurs during wear
  • Provides a 5-part result and saves history to My Shoes

Broader Daily Foot Discomfort

Foot Check

"My feet feel uncomfortable more generally."

Guidance for general foot fatigue, daily standing demands, and routine comfort habits.

  • Evaluates comfort independent of any single pair of shoes
  • Asks about standing duration, floor surfaces, and daily routines
  • Explores discomfort areas and safety screening questions

The Result Experience

Observations you can follow.
Practical next steps.

Every check result is organized into a five-stage progressive sequence—moving from initial observations to practical adjustments and safety guidance.

Explore the result architecture →
01
Observation

What we noticed

A plain-language summary of how your reported shoe details or sensations connect to likely fit or comfort factors.

02
Action

Try first

The lowest-risk, practical starting action—such as lacing technique, posture breaks, or sock pairing.

03
Tools

Helpful tools

Supportive, non-medical aids, fitting pads, or routine ideas that may help your specific situation.

04
Caution

What to avoid

Common missteps—such as blindly sizing up, overtightening laces, or forcing rigid break-ins—that worsen discomfort.

05
Safety

Safety guidance

Clear boundaries advising when to stop adjusting footwear and consult a medical or healthcare professional.

Trust & Responsibility

Clear boundaries.
Honest engineering.

BadgerFit provides educational fit guidance and everyday comfort suggestions. It does not diagnose medical conditions or replace clinical care.

If you experience sharp pain, persistent numbness, tingling, swelling, or symptoms that continue outside your shoes, BadgerFit directs you to seek assessment from an appropriate healthcare professional.

Furthermore, we do not market unvalidated smartphone camera scanning as a finished sizing tool. Foot Scan remains on the workbench in rigorous research until optical accuracy meets physical fitting standards.
