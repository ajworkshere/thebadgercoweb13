The Starting Point

Something about your shoes or feet feels uncomfortable.

A numbered size label only measures length. It leaves width, instep volume, heel shape, and everyday standing fatigue completely unaddressed. When a shoe rubs or feet ache, guessing or sizing up blindly usually creates new problems.

"Sizing up adds length, but it doesn't fix a narrow toe box or a loose heel. Clear answers start by asking better questions."

The Badger Co. Design Principle

Two Focused Paths / Equal Prominence

Choose where your
discomfort begins.

BadgerFit separates problems with a specific pair of shoes from broader foot discomfort. Select the path that matches your current question.

01 / Footwear Specific

Fit Check

"Something feels wrong with this pair." Built for a specific shoe in your rotation that pinches, slips, or feels uncomfortable during wear.

  • Identifies shoe type (dress shoes, boots, sneakers)
  • Focuses on problem zones: heel slipping, toe-box pressure, lace pressure
  • Checks width, arch room, and internal shoe volume
  • Connects directly to your saved shoe closet in My Shoes
Meet Fit Check →
02 / Foot & Routine Focused

Foot Check

"My feet feel uncomfortable more generally." Evaluates broader foot discomfort across daily routines, long hours on your feet, and standing environments.

  • Independent of any single shoe in your rotation
  • Captures daily standing duration, floor surfaces, and shift timing
  • Identifies fatigue patterns, aching arches, and recovery needs
  • Builds your ongoing comfort history in My Foot

The Result Experience

Evidence you can follow.
Practical next steps.

Completing a check doesn't hand you generic advice. BadgerFit connects your reported answers to structured guidance designed to help you make sense of the problem.

Every check result is organized into five clear, actionable parts so you know exactly what to do first, what to keep in mind, and when to stop experimenting.

Explore the result architecture →
Part 01

What we noticed

A plain-language summary of how your reported shoe details or sensations connect to likely fit or comfort factors.

Part 02

Try first

The lowest-risk, practical starting action—such as lacing technique, posture breaks, or sock pairing.

Part 03

Helpful Tools

Supportive, non-diagnostic aids, fitting pads, or contextual recovery ideas that may help your specific situation.

Part 04

What to avoid

Common missteps—such as blindly sizing up, overtightening laces, or forcing rigid break-ins—that worsen discomfort.

Part 05

Safety guidance

Clear escalation boundaries advising when to stop adjusting fit and consult a medical or podiatric professional.

Trust & Responsibility

Clear boundaries.
Honest engineering.

BadgerFit provides educational fit guidance and everyday comfort suggestions. It does not diagnose medical conditions or replace clinical care.

If you experience sharp pain, persistent numbness, tingling, swelling, or symptoms that continue outside your shoes, BadgerFit directs you to seek assessment from an appropriate healthcare professional.

Furthermore, we do not market unvalidated smartphone camera scanning as a finished sizing tool. Foot Scan remains on the workbench in rigorous research until optical accuracy meets physical fitting standards.
